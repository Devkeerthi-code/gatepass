Tickets and the gate, sorted.

What it fixes

The four failures of a Google-Form fest
  1. 01
    Overselling the last seats

    Each checkout holds seats for 10 minutes in Postgres. A CHECK constraint makes "sold more than capacity" impossible.

  2. 02
    Checking UPI screenshots by hand

    Razorpay's signed payment result issues the ticket. If the buyer closes the tab, the signed webhook does it instead — never twice.

  3. 03
    Queues at a printed list

    Volunteers scan with their phone camera: full-screen green, amber or red. A second scan says who let them in and when.

  4. 04
    No idea what's happening

    A live dashboard shows sales per ticket type, revenue, check-ins and arrivals every 15 minutes, plus a CSV for sponsors.

How it stays correct

The parts worth asking about in an interview
Inventory

Atomic seat holds

One conditional UPDATE reserves seats only if they fit. Expired holds are released whenever inventory is read, plus a daily cron. 40 concurrent buyers for 10 seats → exactly 10.

reserved + n <= capacity
Payments

Two paths, one outcome

The checkout callback and the webhook both call one idempotent confirm. Retries, double delivery and both paths racing produce exactly one set of tickets.

PENDING → PAID once
The gate

First scan wins

Check-in only succeeds while the ticket is unscanned. Scanners cache the guest list in IndexedDB, queue scans offline and sync when the signal returns.

checkedInAt IS NULL

Built for three people

The fan

  • Promo codes, group deals and happy-hour prices
  • Ticket page that works offline once loaded
  • Waitlist with a 15-minute offer when seats free up
  • Find tickets again with email + phone

The host

  • Concert builder with live poster and ticket preview
  • Paste Razorpay keys → collect into your own account
  • Mock, Razorpay Test and checklist-gated Live modes
  • One-click refunds, guest list, CSV export
  • Team invites with admin / gate-crew roles

The volunteer

  • Phone camera scanner (installable PWA)
  • Full-screen VALID / ALREADY IN / NOT VALID
  • Search by name, last 4 phone digits or serial
  • Works offline and syncs, conflicts reported
  • Can't open anything except the scanner
